# ADR-002: Voice of Deponent Verbs

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-27
- **Scope:** `src/backend/priscianus/morphology/` and the evals' gold-data comparison

## Context

Wiktionary tags every form of a deponent (*loquor*, *sequor*, *hortor*, *morior*, *utor*) `active`, so *loquitur* is `Voice=Act`. Whitaker's tags the passive-looking forms `PASSIVE`. Both describe the same form (passive in shape, active in meaning). The Whitaker's spot-check (#10) found five forms that differ only in this ([whitakers-spot-check.md](../whitakers-spot-check.md)).

The gold data is not consistent either. Present indicative forms of the five lemmas above in the UD Latin training files (checked 2026-10-27):

| Treebank | `Voice=Act` | `Voice=Pass` |
|---|---|---|
| Perseus | 6 | 5 |
| PROIEL | 1 | 126 |
| ITTB | 0 | 452 |

Perseus uses both for the same lemmas (*sequitur* is `Act` and `Pass`; *loquitur* is `Act`).

## Options considered

| Option | Benefits | Downsides |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Keep `Voice=Act`; treat Act and Pass as equivalent for deponents when comparing.** | No remapping. The index stays traceable to Wiktionary. Handles Perseus's mix and the Pass-heavy PROIEL and ITTB. | Comparisons need to know which lemmas are deponent. A wrong voice claim on a deponent is not caught. |
| **B. Remap passive-looking deponent forms to `Voice=Pass` at build time.** | Matches Whitaker's, PROIEL and ITTB. | Needs a deponent-lemma list and a rule for which forms look passive. That is a rule written in this repo, which ADR-001 tries to avoid. Disagrees with Perseus's `Act` rows. |
| **C. Keep `Voice=Act` and compare strictly.** | Simplest. | Every passive-looking deponent form disagrees with Whitaker's and with most gold data. |

## Decision

**Option A.** The index keeps Wiktionary's `Voice=Act`. Where a comparison against another source involves a deponent, `Voice` is not compared.

- In the Whitaker's spot-check, an analysis is deponent when its dictionary entry ends in ` sum` (*loquor, loqui, locutus sum*). `Voice` is dropped from the expected features, as gender on nouns and `Degree=Pos` already are.
- Non-deponent verbs are still compared on voice. *amatur* must still be passive.

## Consequences

- The five forms in #31 now agree; the spot-check goes from 99 to 104 of 130.
- Semi-deponents (*audeo, audere, ausus sum*) share the ` sum` marker, so their voice is also not compared. Their active-shaped forms are active in both sources, so nothing is hidden today, but a wrong passive claim on one would pass.
- **Open for the verifier and evals.** The index does not record that a lemma is deponent. When the verifier checks a claim of voice against gold data, it will need that flag (for example from Wiktionary's `deponent` conjugation tag) to apply the same rule. Do this in the issue that builds the gold-data comparison.
